FLAGSHIP ENGINEERING ANALYSIS

Cashew Nut Shell Splitting Machine

METALLURGICAL CORROSION MITIGATION & BLADE LIFE EXTENSION
STATUS ● VALIDATED DESIGN

In commercial cashew nut processing, high moisture environments combined with acidic Cashew Nut Shell Liquid (CNSL) subject cutting blades to aggressive premature wear and electrochemical corrosion. This project systematically targeted blade longevity through informed metallurgical selection.

01 // Problem

Rapid blade corrosion and premature edge dulling under continuous moisture and caustic CNSL contact, causing frequent line stoppages and blade replacements.

02 // Analysis

Examined chemical degradation and pitting wear patterns on conventional high-carbon tool steels, identifying galvanic susceptibility in moist plant conditions.

03 // Solution

Recommended replacing standard blade material with high-molybdenum marine-grade steel (AISI 316 / marine stainless) engineered for severe corrosion resistance.

04 // Outcome

Achieved vastly superior resistance to corrosive fluid degradation, significantly extended expected blade operational lifespan, and reduced machine downtime.

TECHNICAL SPECS: Marine-Grade Stainless Alloy // Corrosion Resistance // Mechanical Kinematics [ROI: REDUCED TOOLING OVERHEAD]
